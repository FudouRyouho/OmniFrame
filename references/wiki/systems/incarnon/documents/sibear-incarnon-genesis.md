---

## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/SlamsCreateIceFields(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Create a <DT_COLD> Blast Radius with Heavy Slams.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+50%** Heavy Attack Wind Up Speed
- **+10%** Sprint Speed
- **+10%** to Parkour Velocity

**Notas:**
- Heavy slams create a slowing field over the radius of the slam for **4** seconds.
