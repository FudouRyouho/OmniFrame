## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/HeavyAtkVoidSpikesComboCt(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Heavy Slam attacks impale nearby enemies on Void Spikes.
- **+3** Range
- **-35%** Max Melee Attack Speed
- **+100%** of <DT_IMPACT> Damage converted to <DT_PUNCTURE> Damage

**Notas:**
- Heavy Slam attacks impale enemies within **8 meters** on Void spikes for **1.5s + 1.0s * (combo multiplier - 1)**, for a maximum total duration of **12.5 seconds**.
- The height from which the slam is performed does not affect the impale radius.
- Melee Attack Speed curse is multiplicative to all other attack speed buffs.

**Challenge:** Kill **100** enemies with the Ruvox.

## EVO 5

**Challenge:** Complete a Solo mission with an Incarnon Weapon equipped in every slot.

### Brutal Efficiency

![brutal_efficiency](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/HeavyEffOnMultImpaled(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Gain **+40%** Heavy Attack Efficiency for **20s** when impaling **5** or more enemies.

### Vulnerability Serum

![vulnerability_serum](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/ImpaledStatusVuln(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Impaled enemies are **+35%** more vulnerable to Status Chance

**Notas:**
- The status chance boost is a **flat 35%**, allowing damage sources with **0%** status chance to inflict status procs.
- **Any** source of damage can benefit from status chance vulnerability, not just Ruvox. This includes abilities.
- AoE Status Effects from other enemies such as <DT_ELECTRICITY> can trigger new status effects on vulnerable enemies, allowing exponential damage growth with prolonged chaining.

### Permanent Perforation

![permanent_perforation](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/ImpaledPuncProcCrit(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Enemies suffer **5** <DT_PUNCTURE> Status while impaled
