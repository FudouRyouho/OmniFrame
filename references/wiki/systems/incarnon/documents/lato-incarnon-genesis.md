# Lato — Incarnon Genesis

> **Variantes:** Lato · Lato Vandal · Lato Prime

---

## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormRicochetOnHittingEnemies(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Gain Punch Through and Ricochet.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form gains increased base damage, Critical Chance, Critical Multiplier, and overall Status Chance. It also gains an innate Multishot of 2 (similar to shotguns) and 0.8 meter Punch Through. Shots also ricochet to **1** nearby enemy within **10** meters (**12** meters for the prime) of the main target, plus any subsequent targets struck via punch through. However, it has slower Fire Rate and significantly higher Recoil.
- Modding for Punch Through and hitting multiple targets will create successive ricochets.
- <DT_VOID> Status Effect prevents ricochets from occurring.

### Reified Bane

![reified_bane](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/ReloadFromEmpty(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X**.
- On Reload from Empty Magazine: Increase Base Damage by **+Y**.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Lato | X = 30 / Y = 30 |
| Lato Vandal | X = 12 / Y = 30 |
| Lato Prime | X = 2 / Y = 24 |

**Notas:**
- Once the damage bonus has been earned, the ONLY way to lose it is to manually reload with a non-empty magazine.
