## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormNapalmGrenadeLauncher(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Fire high-powered grenades.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form becomes a semi-automatic grenade launcher whose projectiles explode in a **7.9** meter radius dealing <DT_IMPACT> damage on direct hits and <DT_BLAST> damage on the explosion, with significantly higher base damage, Critical Chance, Critical Multiplier, and Status Chance. However it has significantly slower Fire Rate, the explosion possesses linear Damage Falloff from 100% to 20% from central impact, and the projectile has travel time with arcing affected by Projectile Speed.
