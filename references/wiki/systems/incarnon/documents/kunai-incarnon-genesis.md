# Kunai — Incarnon Genesis

> **Variantes:** Kunai · Mk1-Kunai

---

## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/AddMultishotAndKunaiSeekHeads(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Projectiles seek Headshots and have increased Multishot.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form grants its projectiles head-homing properties, with increased base damage weighted towards <DT_SLASH>, Critical Chance, and Critical Multiplier, and 2 base Multishot.
- Punch Through is ignored in Incarnon Form and will not cause projectiles to home in on additional targets.
- Concealed Explosives is disabled in Incarnon Form.

## EVO 4

**Challenge:** Kill **60** enemies within **10m**

### Deathtrap Trigger

![deathtrap_trigger](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/OnEquipCritChanceDamageOnFirstAttack(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- On equip from Primary:
- Increase Base Critical Chance by **+X%** for **4s**.
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by **+Y** for **4s**.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Kunai | X = 40% / Y = 1.4x |
| Mk1-Kunai | X = 42% / Y = 1x |

**Notas:**
- Bug: These are displayed as permanent bonuses in the Arsenal but only actually apply while the 4 second Deathtrap Trigger buff is active.
