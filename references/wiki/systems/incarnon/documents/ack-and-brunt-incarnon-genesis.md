## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/HeavyAttacksSendOutSpectralAxeAndShield(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Send forth a spectral axe during aerial and slide attacks.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+20%** Sprint Speed
- **+20%** to Parkour Velocity

**Notas:**
- Spectral axes deal **149** damage on contact, then explode dealing **303** <DT_HEAT> damage with a forced <DT_IMPACT> status effect in a **5m** radius. Axes launched by aerial attacks have **2x** damage.
- <DT_HEAT> damage will combine with modded elements (for example, an Ack & Brunt modded for <DT_ELECTRICITY> will throw <DT_RADIATION> axes).
- Spectral axe hits do **not** add to the melee combo counter.
- Direct hits of spectral axes and explosions are affected by universal base damage sources like Vigorous Swap, Holster Amp, Arcane Arachne, Vex Armor and Amp.
- Direct hits of spectral axes are affected by Condition Overload, while the explosions are not.
- Direct hits of spectral axes and explosions are **not** affected by the damage increase on Evolution II perks or melee damage bonuses like Incarnon form's innate +100% melee damage, Pressure Point, and Arcane Fury.
- Lateral slam attack is **replaced** by the axe throw, reducing mobility. Slam attacks can only be performed directly downwards while Incarnon is active.
