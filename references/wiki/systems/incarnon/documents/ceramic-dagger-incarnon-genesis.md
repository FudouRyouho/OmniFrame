## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/HeavyAttacksThrowSpectralDaggers(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Heavy Attacks sends forth spectral daggers.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+25%** Sprint Speed
- **+25%** to Parkour Velocity

**Notas:**
- Heavy Attack in Incarnon Form will fire a flurry of **2** spectral daggers towards the crosshair that explode on impact, dealing <DT_HEAT> damage with a forced <DT_IMPACT> status effect.
- Direct hits can headshot.
- Projectiles and explosions are silent.
- Direct hits of spectral daggers and explosions are affected by universal base damage sources like Vigorous Swap, Holster Amp, Arcane Arachne, Vex Armor and Amp.
- Direct hit damage is affected by Condition Overload, but not the explosions.
- Damage is **not** affected by the melee damage increase on Evolution 2, Incarnon form's innate +100% melee damage, or melee damage bonuses like Pressure Point.
