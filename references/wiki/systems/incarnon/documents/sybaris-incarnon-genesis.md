# Sybaris — Incarnon Genesis

> **Variantes:** Sybaris · Dex Sybaris · Sybaris Prime

---

## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/4RoundInnateBlast.png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Fire 4-round bursts and gain <DT_BLAST> Status

**Notas:**
- Burst count is increased to 4 and shots have guaranteed <DT_BLAST> proc. Damage, Critical Multiplier, and Status Chance are increased, but Critical Chance is reduced.

![blazing_barrel](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/MultishotCostsAmmoTakenFromPoolNotClip.png)

|----------|-------|

| Dex Sybaris | X = 15 |

**Notas:**

## EVO 4

**Challenge:** Get **40** weakpoint hits with this weapon in Incarnon form in a single mission.

### Elemental Dominance

![elemental_dominance](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/GainStatusChanceOfCritChance.png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Status Chance by +**X** Effect doubles in Incarnon Form.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Sybaris | X = 15% |
| Dex Sybaris | X = 15% |
| Sybaris Prime | X = 8% |

**Notas:**
- Sybaris Prime gains **+23%** Status Chance in Incarnon form, rather than the expected **+16%**.

### Reaver's Rapture

![reavers_rapture](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/DamageIncreaseOnFullBurstHitResetOnReload.png)

**Efectos:**
- On Full Burst Hit: **+20%** Damage, resets on Reload

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Sybaris | - |
| Dex Sybaris | - |
| Sybaris Prime | - |

**Notas:**
- Stacks up to 4x.
- This can be bypassed by using  Blood Forge, but not effects that reload the weapon while holstered such as  Lock and Load.
