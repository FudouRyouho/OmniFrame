## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/OkinaGhosts(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Kills generate spectral daggers that target enemies, applying max <DT_COLD> Status stacks.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+20%** Sprint Speed
- **+20%** to Parkour Velocity

**Notas:**
- Kills create a dagger that orbits the player, then seek an enemy within **15** meters, applying 10 <DT_COLD> stacks to the target, then exploding in a **4m** radius and applying 1 <DT_COLD> stack to nearby enemies.
- Up to **6** daggers can be active at once.
- Spectral dagger hits do **not** add to the melee combo counter.
- Direct hits of spectral daggers and explosions are affected by universal base damage sources.
- Direct hits of spectral daggers are affected by Condition Overload, while the explosions are not.
- Condition Overload effects are multiplicative to sources of universal base damage.
- Direct hits of spectral daggers and explosions are **not** affected by melee damage bonuses like Incarnon form's innate +100% melee damage, Pressure Point, and Arcane Fury.
- Spectral Daggers can activate Melee Influence's buff, but do not benefit from it.
- Kills from the spectral daggers do not generate new daggers, but kills from Melee Influence do.
