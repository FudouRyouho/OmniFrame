# Sicarus — Incarnon Genesis

> **Variantes:** Sicarus · Prime
> Raw: sicarus-incarnon-genesis.wikitext

## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormRicochetOnHittingEnemies(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Bullets ricochet.

**Notas:**
- Base damage, base Critical Chance, and base Critical Multiplier are increased. Sicarus also gains increased base Status Chance.
- Shots ricochet to **1** nearby enemy within **6** meters of the main target and any subsequent targets struck by punch through.
- Ricochets **can** hit weakpoints, but not reliably.

## EVO 2

**Challenge:** Complete a solo mission with this weapon equipped

### Feigned Retreat

![feigned_retreat](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/XDamageToEnemiesAtFullHealthAndBelowHalfHealth(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X.**
- **+40%** Damage to enemies below half Health.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Sicarus | X = 50 |
| Prime | X = 40 |

**Notas:**
- Bonus damage is additive with mods such as  Hornet Strike but does not take into account the Base Damage increase from this perk.

### King's Gambit

![kings_gambit](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/BodyShotsCantCritHeadshotsIncCritChance(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X** (Sicarus) / **+X** (Prime)
- **x0** Critical Chance on Bodyshots, **+150%** Critical Chance on Weakpoint Hits.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Sicarus | X = 50 |
| Prime | X = 40 |

**Notas:**
- Bodyshot modifier is multiplicative with all sources of Critical Chance, effectively making non-headshot critical hits impossible.
- Citrine's Crystallize is an exception to this.
- Weakpoint modifier is additive with mods such as Pistol Gambit

### Wiseman's Regard

![wisemans_regard](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/GainStatusChanceOfCritChance(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Status Chance by **30%** of current Critical Chance, up to **40%**

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Sicarus | - |
| Prime | - |

**Notas:**
- Maximum Base Status Chance increase is gained at **134%** Critical Chance, achievable with **+734%** (Sicarus) / **+434%** (Prime) modded Critical Chance, or **+567%** (Sicarus) / **+345%** (Prime) in Incarnon Form.
- Incarnon Form Status Chance is displayed in the Arsenal screen as if using the normal form's Critical Chance, but will properly use its' own Critical chance while in-mission.
- In addition to regular Critical Chance mods, it is also affected by both parts of Galvanized Crosshairs while aiming, Secondary Outburst, Cascadia Overcharge, Arcane Avenger, Secondary Enervate, Yareli's Passive, Zephyr's Passive, Covenant's baseline Critical Chance bonus but *not* the 4x multiplier on headshots, and Cat's Eye.
- **Not** affected by the **King's Gambit** Evolution II perk, Cascadia Accuracy, Crystallize, or Shadow Haze.
