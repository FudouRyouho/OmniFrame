## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/SlideRangeSlashBias(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Incarnon form temporarily increases Range and inflicts guaranteed <DT_SLASH> Status for all attacks after a successful slide attack.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+20%** Sprint Speed
- **+20%** Bullet Jump

**Notas:**
- Successful slide attacks grant **+3** Range and forced <DT_SLASH> Status for all attacks for **6s**.
