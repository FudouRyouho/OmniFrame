## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/SeekingFireballs(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Direct hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Launches target-seeking fireballs that ricochet towards enemies.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form becomes fully automatic and shoots small fireballs that deal pure <DT_HEAT> damage. Upon hitting an enemy, the projectile will ricochet toward **1** enemy within **20** meters of the initial target. This ricochet has no homing capability and can miss. Projectiles that hit a surface can bounce up to **2** times before dissipating on the third impact, but these ricochets do not automatically aim towards enemies.
