## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormFatBeamOfFire(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Fire a wide <DT_HEAT> Damage beam.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form fires a short-ranged, wide Klamora Prism-like beam that deals pure <DT_HEAT> damage and has significantly increased base damage, Critical Chance, Critical Multiplier, Status Chance, and Fire Rate, but has linear Damage Falloff from 100% to 80% from 10m to 16m target distance and has a maximum range of **16** meters.
- Due to not being able to be equipped with Ruinous Extension, its damage falloff is affected by Projectile Speed, but the beam length isn't. Thus, it is possible to completely negate falloff with sufficient Projectile Speed.
- The beam has no innate punch-through effect.
