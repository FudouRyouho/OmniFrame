## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/VoidScytheTier0(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Attacks embed a damaging shard in enemies. Each time a new shard is created, existing shards deal **75%** of weapon damage to embedded foes.
- **+3** Range
- **+40%** Attack Speed

**Notas:**
- Light attacks (not slam attacks or heavy attacks) embed a shard in enemies which lasts for **15** seconds. Each enemy can only be embedded with **one** shard at a time.
- Embedding a shard deals no damage to the main target, but triggers all shards embedded in other enemies, dealing damage to their host.
- Shards can be triggered regardless of the distance between the newly embedded enemy and any previously embedded enemies.
- Triggering a shard does not remove it from its host and it can still be triggered by new shards.
- Shard trigger damage is **75% of Thalys's base damage**, for a total of 157.5 Slash Damage and 67.5 Puncture Damage.
- If the triggering attack has a damage multiplier from a melee stance combo, it will also multiply the shard damage.
- **Is** affected by base damage bonuses such as Pressure Point and Vex Armor, but not Condition Overload.
- **Is not** affected by IPS, elemental, or faction damage bonuses. Does not trigger forced procs from Valence Formation or Toxic Lash.
- **0% status chance**, can be increased with flat bonuses like Proton Snap and Shroud of Dynar.
- Status duration **is not** affected by Lasting Sting.
- **0% critical chance**, can be increased with flat bonuses like Arcane Avenger and Wrathful Advance.
- **1.0x critical multiplier**, affected by mods like Organ Shatter and Melee Doughty.
- Melee Doughty's bonus scales with Thalys's normal melee stats, not the shard stats, allowing the shards to gain a significant critical multiplier.
- Cannot trigger Melee Duplicate even when the shards themselves crit.
- Shard damage is considered an instance of Unattributed Damage.

**Challenge:** Kill **100** enemies with the Thalys.

## EVO 5

**Challenge:** Complete a Solo mission with an Incarnon Weapon equipped in every slot.

### Explosive Growth

![explosive_growth](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/GrowingShards(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Shards grow when a new shard is created, up to **3** times. Fully grown shards erupt out of the embedded enemy, dealing **x2** damage and impaling them on the spot.

**Notas:**
- New shards still trigger existing shards to deal damage, in addition to the growth effect.
- Shard growth is limited to 1 stage per melee swing, regardless of how many new enemies are embedded with a shard. However, a shard **can** be embedded and grow by 1 stage in the same melee swing.
- Impaled enemies are stunned for 5 seconds while suspended in the air. After the impale ends, the enemy will fall to ground before taking a second to stand back up.
- Impaling an enemy removes the shard on them. They cannot be embedded with a new shard until the impale is over.
- Impale damage is 2x the normal shard trigger damage, but otherwise has the same stats and is affected by the same mods.
- Condition Overload is an exception, as it affects the impale damage but not the shard damage (likely a bug).
- Impale effect does not trigger Melee Afflictions despite being somewhat similar to <DT_LIFTED>.

### Chain Shatter

![chain_shatter](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/HeavyAttackDetonateShards(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Heavy attacks detonate shards. Shards caught within the blast radius will also detonate

**Notas:**
- Heavy slams will also detonate shards.
- Detonated shards deal **200% of Thalys's base damage** (420 Slash and 180 Puncture) to their host.
- Has a damaging radius of only about ~**1m** with ?% falloff.
- The detonation will also trigger other shards within ~**5m** to detonate, which can cause a chain reaction.
- Combo multiplier will only multiply the detonation damage to the shard's host and enemies in the 1m damaging radius. Other shards detonated by the explosion will **not** benefit from the combo multiplier.
- **Is** affected by base damage bonuses (including Condition Overload), as well as IPS and elemental bonuses (unlike the shard trigger damage).
- **Is not** affected by faction damage bonuses.
- Has **24% status chance**, 0% crit chance, and a 1x critical multiplier.
- Does not trigger forced procs from Valence Formation or Toxic Lash.
- **Can** trigger Melee Influence.
- **Cannot** trigger Melee Duplicate.
- Enemies that have been detonated are still able to have a new shard embedded in them by further melee attacks, allowing them to be detonated again.

### Void Splinters

![void_splinters](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/LongLastingShards(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Shard duration is increased to **30s**. Shards become weak spots that have **+100%** Critical Chance when damaged. Shard damage is reduced by **-50%**.

**Notas:**
- Critical Chance bonus is additive to mods such as Point Strike, Pistol Gambit and True Steel.
