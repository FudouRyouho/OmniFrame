## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/KillsLeaveAPoolOfToxicWaste(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Kills create a <DT_TOXIN> Damage field
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+20%** Sprint Speed
- **+20%** to Bullet Jump

**Notas:**
- Fields do **350** <DT_TOXIN> damage (increased to **440** by EVO2) and last for **7** seconds.
- Fields will be created by any kills made Dual Ichor's attacks or status damage, both from the weapon itself and from other fields that have been created. This allows for fields to create other fields in a loop so long as they continue getting kills before disappearing.
- Fields have **0%** Critical Chance, **1x** Critical Damage, **4m** range and **50%** Status Chance.
- Critical Chance **cannot** be increased, even with flat buffs like Arcane Avenger, Cat's Eye, or Wrathful Advance, or enemy debuffs like Shadow Haze.
- Range is **not** affected by melee range mods like Reach.
- Status Chance is **not** affected by mods (including Proton Snap) or the **Absolute Valor** evolution.
- Status Duration can be increased, such as by Lasting Sting.
- Damage is affected by melee damage buffs such as Pressure Point and Incarnon form's innate **+100%** Melee Damage.
- Clouds are **not** affected by Condition Overload.
- Damage is affected by all elemental damage buffs such as Fever Strike and Molten Impact, increasing its damage and changing its damage type as expected.
- Damage is affected by faction damage bonuses such as Smite Grineer, and will correctly double dip on status procs made by the fields.
- Damage is affected by status damage buffs such as Melee Elementalist.
- Damage is affected by Stealth Damage Bonus (+700%).
- Statuses inflicted by fields will **not** be spread by Melee Influence.
