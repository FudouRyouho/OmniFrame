## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/SingleShotChargeExplosiveHit(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Charge a large single shot that deals Radial <DT_HEAT> Damage.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form shoots charged explosive lasers with a **0.6** second charge time, inflicts pure <DT_HEAT> damage on the explosion, and explodes in a **6** meter radius, with increased base damage, Critical Chance, and Status Chance. However direct hits no longer deals <DT_SLASH> damage, it has significantly slower Fire Rate, and the explosion possesses linear Damage Falloff from 100% to 60% from central impact.
- Incarnon Form trigger must be fully charged to fire.

## EVO 2

**Challenge:** Complete a solo mission with this weapon equipped

### Precision's Payoff

![precisions_payoff](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/OnHeadshotFromFullBurst(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X**.
- On Burst Headshots: Increase Base Damage by **+20**. Stacks up to **3x**.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Zylok | X = 76 |
| Prime | X = 30 |

**Notas:**
- Stacks are generated if both shots from holding and releasing Zylok's trigger are headshots.
- Stacks are shown as a buff, which are lost on reload or switching to Incarnon Form.
- Incarnon Form cannot trigger burst headshots, as it has a Charge trigger instead of Duplex.

### Mauler's Magazine

![maulers_magazine](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/CritDamageOnReloadFromEmpty(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X**.
- On Reload From Empty: Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by **+1x**. Stacks up to **2x**.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Zylok | X = 76 |
| Prime | X = 30 |

**Notas:**
- Stacks are shown as a buff, which are lost when reloading from a partial magazine or on death.
