## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/ShortRangeBeams(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Fire high power beams and gain <DT_MAGNETIC> Damage.

**Notas:**
- Fires lasers that inflict <DT_MAGNETIC> damage. Gains 3 Punch Through and Damage, Critical Chance, and Critical Multiplier are increased, but Status Chance and Fire Rate are lowered.
- Despite the Incarnon form description saying it fires beams, it is not a Beam Weapon.
