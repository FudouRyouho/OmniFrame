## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormMultishotSlowerFireRate(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Gain increased Status Chance and Multishot while slowing fire rate.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form transforms into a full-auto shotgun with tight spread weighted slightly more towards <DT_SLASH> damage, **8** base Multishot, and increased base damage, Critical Multiplier, and overall Status Chance. However, it has reduced Critical Chance and Fire Rate and a very low Status Chance per pellet (2.5% for base, 3% for prime).
- Incarnon Form can trigger Hata-Satya.
- Switching to and from Incarnon Form resets Hata-Satya's bonus.
