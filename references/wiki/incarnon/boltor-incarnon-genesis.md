## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormShrapnelMultishotIncreasedSlash(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Prioritize <DT_SLASH> Damage and gain Multishot.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form turns the weapon in a rapid-fire pseudo-shotgun with **3** base multishot, increased overall damage stats, added <DT_SLASH> damage, and slightly faster fire rate, but with lower accuracy resulting in a shotgun-like spread pattern.
