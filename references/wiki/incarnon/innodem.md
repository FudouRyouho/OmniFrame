## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/EvolutionIncarnonFormBlade.png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **5x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Aerial Attacks while Aim Gliding launch <DT_RADIATION> Damage projectiles, and Finishers increase Damage Reduction.
- **+3** Range
- **+40%** Attack Speed

**Notas:**
- Aim glide melee attacks launch projectiles which deal 360 <DT_RADIATION> damage.
- Projectile has infinite Punch Through.
- Projectile has a range of 21 meters.
- Projectiles do not contribute to but benefit from Melee Combo Counter.
- Finishers grant **Incarnon Resilience**, increasing Damage Reduction by **10%** up to four stacks. These  and last for the duration of Incarnon Form.
- Mercy finishers with a Parazon do not apply a stack of **Incarnon Resilience**.

**Challenge:** Kill **100** enemies with the Innodem.

## EVO 3

**Challenge:** Complete **3** Heavy Attacks on Thrax Centurions or Legates

### Hawk Momentum

![hawk_momentum](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/EvolutionHawkMomentum(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Gain **5** Combo for every 10m of continuous slide

**Notas:**
- Only counts slide distance while grounded.
- Weapon must be in hand for perk to apply.

### Skyborne Hunter

![skyborne_hunter](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/EvolutionSkyborneHunter(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase range of aerial melee attacks by **+0.5m**.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form projectile and <DT_SLASH>, <DT_HEAT>, and <DT_TOXIN> Status Effect kills while airborne count towards generating stacks.

### Blade Twister

![blade_twister](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/EvolutionBladeTwister(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
