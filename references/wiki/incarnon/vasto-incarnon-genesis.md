## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/HammerFanBurstShotgun(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Weakpoint hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.
- Rapidly empty a full magazine.

**Notas:**
- Incarnon Form transforms into a 6-round burst with **6** base multishot along with increased Critical Chance and Critical Multiplier. However, it has reduced base damage and Status Chance per bullet, has significantly higher Recoil, and cannot Zoom.
