## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncarnonFormEfficientHeavySlamsWithDamageField(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Incarnon Form gains Heavy Slam Efficiency and leaves behind <DT_HEAT> Damage fields after Heavy Slams.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+25%** Sprint Speed
- **+25%** to Bullet Jump

**Notas:**
- Heavy Slams receive **90%** Heavy Attack Efficiency.
- Field does **50** <DT_HEAT> damage every second for **7** seconds.
- Field has **0%** Critical Chance, **1x** Critical Damage, **5m** range and **50%** Status Chance.
- Status Chance is not affected by the **Absolute Dominion** evolution or mods.
- Field damage is affected by elemental mods, melee damage mods, and faction mods.
- Field damage is not affected by combo multiplier.
- Field's radius is not affected by range mods such as Reach or the **Moonrise Velocity** evolution.
- Field can trigger and benefit from Melee Influence.

### Swordsman's Flourish

![swordsmans_flourish](../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/DamageAndComboGainInSwordAloneMode(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X**.
- With Melee Weapon Equipped: **+100%** Combo Count Chance

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Furax | X = 110 |
| Mk1-Furax | X = 100 |
| Furax Wraith | X = 65 |

**Notas:**
- Combo Count Chance bonus requires manually equipping the melee, either by holding the weapon swap key (default ) or going into a mission with only the melee weapon equipped.
- Combo Count Chance bonus does not affect quickswap melee attacks.
