# Bo — Incarnon Genesis

> **Variantes:** Bo · Mk1-Bo · Bo Prime
> Raw: bo-incarnon-genesis.wikitext

---

## EVO 1 — Incarnon Form

![incarnon_form](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/IncreaseRangeAndHeavyAttackEfficiency(xWhite).png)

**Descripción:**
- Reach **6x** Combo and then Heavy Attack to activate Incarnon Form.
- Incarnon form increases Range and Heavy Attack Efficiency.
- **+100%** Melee Damage
- **+4** Range
- **+50%** Heavy Attack Efficiency
- **+20%** Sprint Speed
- **+20%** to Bullet Jump

## EVO 2

**Challenge:** Complete a solo mission with this weapon equipped

### Stalwart Oak

![stalwart_oak](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/DmgAndIncreasedBlockAngleComboFromBlockedShotsWArmor(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X**.
- With Armor over **450**:
- **+30** Parry Angle
- **+300%** Combo Count Chance while Blocking.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Bo | X = 110 |
| Mk1-Bo | X = 90 |
| Bo Prime | X = 34 |

### Swordsman's Flourish

![swordsmans_flourish](../../../../../Project/public/assets/incarnon-evolutions/DamageAndComboGainInSwordAloneMode(xWhite).png)

**Efectos:**
- Increase Base Damage by **+X**.
- With Melee Weapon Equipped: **+100%** Combo Count Chance.

| Variante | Valor |
|----------|-------|
| Bo | X = 100 |
| Mk1-Bo | X = 85 |
| Bo Prime | X = 30 |

**Notas:**
- Combo Count Chance bonus requires manually equipping the melee, either by holding the weapon swap key (default ) or going into a mission with only the melee weapon equipped.
- Combo Count Chance bonus does not affect quickswap melee attacks.
